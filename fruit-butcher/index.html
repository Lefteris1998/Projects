helpHow to play
  • Click/touch and drag to slash. Several fruit in one swipe = gore combo.
  • Let a fruit fall off-screen and you lose a ❤. Five strikes and you're out.
  • Bombs cost a ❤ but blow up every fruit on screen (you get the points) and blast the blood off your screen.
  • Blood sticks to the screen. Lemons 🍋 trigger the windshield wiper, and it kicks in on its own if you can barely see.
  • Organs that fall out can be sliced again for bonus points. More slicing, more blood. Choose wisely.
  • Weapons each slash differently: the knife keeps your screen cleaner, the cleaver is gorier, the katana reaches furthest, Ea tears shockwaves. Shadowmourne reaps a soul from every kill; at 10 souls, Chaos Bane sends a ghost army after the fruit.
  • Level 5 ends with a boss that spits fruit at you. Fruit it shoots still cost a ❤ if they get away.
  • Keys: P pause · M mute · ] skip level · B throw a bomb · S +1 soul (playtest cheats)
